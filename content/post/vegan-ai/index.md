*All of this is my essay, not based on any research, written out by hand, to express my thoughts on the topic I found curious.

I keep finding people across the web, in one way or another promoting some sort of Vegan-AI or zero-tolerancy-AI policy, let's coin it as VegAI. This applies to art, code and really whatever, with more and less intuitive examples.

To understand this better, let's view this phenomenon from multiple angles:

1. Final consumer's perspective
2. Personal fear and believes
3. Ecology
4. Intellect part of AI
5. Joining counter-force, moral and feeling of mass

## Final consumer

From the final consumer's perspective, there are two distinct cases: indirect and direct.

### Indirect
AI was used in the backend that doesn't directly make way into final product - research, ideas, critics, testing, pre-production, MVP-development. Final user cannot physically tell whether or not AI was used in any way.

In this scenario AI in genuinely simply one of the tools used to in development: user can implicitly detect it in company's stock price, product price, quality and quantity of produce.

On paper AI, like any other tool of automation, should increase interest of investors, decrease price and/or increase quality and/or quantity.

Practically, AI, again, just like any other automation tool, pleases people's laziness, keeping produce at the same level by dropping their workload, which is also a valid outcome of automation.

### Direct

AI-generated videos, texts, images and games, which may look, sound or feel wrong, out of place, artifical and deeply uncomfortable.

Code is not the final product on its own - players, app or website users don't see or appreciate code and cannot know who or what made it. Bugs, breaches and problems make code quality stand out of course, but it happens anyway and doesn't make code more of a final product. Nobody can, by using a product, tell whether or not AI was used at some point of creating it.

AI increases quantity of products, increasing also the quantity of problems because of average competence dropping. Just like a factory of people who only know how to do their role cannot out-perform a standalone professional in quality, but in quantity.

---

In any case, direct or indirect, AI brings more incompetent people to the market, increasing the amount of low-quality (as well as high-quality, but less so) produce. Companies cutting costs makes them more money, their produce becomes more affordable, but potential workers (artists, programmers and whoever) were the cut cost.

## Personal fear or believes

Everyone is familiar with this. We are afraid of being replaced by heartless machines and greedy ignorants who would do whatever for money. We are afraid of becoming useless, of the world dropping to the abyss, of art disappearing and humans becoming poisoned, demoralized and depedent, not being able to reason, think or perform themselves.

## Ecology

Anyway, the point is that all of this "training" - showing texts and images over and over - is an expensive process: there are millions of texts, articles, websites, images and videos on the internet, when even taking one is a sophisticated computational process. It is a technical miracle that all of this is possible, but the sheer amount of resources put - and continues to be put - into it is concerning. Electricity, water, noise and silicon are needed in immense amounts.

## Intellect

AI is not a regular program or algorithm, it's a massive mathematical structure that holds billions and trillions of numbers. Each seen text or image adjusts many of those numbers a little bit. After months of such process, those numbers happen to align in such way that it can predict texts and convert text to images and back.

It doesn't understand crap about them, there is no "understanding" in those numbers in principle - it just knows that after "I love" usually comes "you", and that it usually appears in context of conversation between close people. Those contexts and idea of people is also entirely based on what input it was given and how it seemed to be organized. Still, no idea of WHY this happens it doesn't have - it can write about it, also from what it saw - the explanations it read about what love is and how it's expressed. Paradoxically, it can explain it without understanding it.

Initiaveness is not something out of its league, counter intuitive for many people. It's not rocket science to make it run in the background and do whatever, occasionally coming up with something.

## Being a part of something bigger

Joining something big on behalf of common belief is always empowering. This movement might balance the economy
